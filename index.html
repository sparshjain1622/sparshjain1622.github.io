About SJ

I am Sparsh Jain. People call me SJ. I build things, I climb things, and I talk to strangers about their dreams until the chai goes cold.

I grew up and graduated in Indore. The early years were not easy: family pressure, work pressure, money pressure, all at once. I learned to stay calm in the middle of it, and that calm became my superpower.

Since then I have freelanced, worked in teams, led teams, and built products of my own: a travel platform, digital helpers that do real work, a voice assistant for my home, and two free books about what I learned.

When I am not building, I am on a mountain, in a river, or at a stove making dal for friends. I say yes to challenges, because every yes has taken me somewhere better.

  • ProactiveI would rather try and learn than wait and wonder.
